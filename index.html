Biography & Mission

Dedicated to Youth Welfare, Academic Honesty & Grassroots Service

Aayush Kashyap is a dynamic student leader and committed activist working at the forefront of student welfare under the banner of Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP), Chapra City Unit (Bihar). Known for his tireless advocacy across Jai Prakash University (JPU) and surrounding constituent colleges, Aayush represents the hopes, demands, and democratic rights of thousands of aspiring students.

"हमारा उद्देश्य केवल समस्याओं को उठाना नहीं, बल्कि व्यवस्था में सकारात्मक व निर्णायक सुधार लाना है। शिक्षा के मंदिर में भ्रष्टाचार, अनियमित सत्र और छात्र उपेक्षा के विरुद्ध संघर्ष जारी रहेगा।"

Beyond campus agitations, his activism emphasizes constructive nation-building—organizing community cleanliness drives (Swachhata Abhiyan), blood donation camps, environmental initiatives, and counseling sessions for students arriving from rural parts of Saran district.
